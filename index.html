Heavenly Foretaste is a Seventh-day Adventist self-supporting health ministry rooted in Nontshinga Village, Kentani, in the Eastern Cape.

Our retreat centre is being established, while our ministry continues through retreats, education and community outreach.

Our Story & Purpose

About Heavenly Foretaste

A faith-rooted ministry born from a vision to transform lives across Africa through God's natural principles of healing and wholeness.

Who We Are

Healing Lives,
God's Way

Heavenly Foretaste Lifestyle Retreat is a Seventh-day Adventist self-supporting health ministry rooted in Nontshinga Village, Kentani, Eastern Cape. Founded by Zezethu Vellem, the ministry exists to bridge the gap between disease and wholeness — offering people God's natural path to healing rather than dependence on pharmaceuticals.

We holistically heal and transform people's lives in Africa by providing comprehensive health education, addressing physical wellbeing, nurturing spiritual renewal, and emotionally revitalising guests through nature's beauty in our social atmosphere of love.

Vision

We holistically heal and transform people's lives in Africa by providing comprehensive health education, addressing physical wellbeing, nurturing spiritual renewal while emotionally vitalized by nature's beauty in our social atmosphere of love.

Mission

To be the best health retreat that offers restorative natural health treatments, education, and healthy whole food meals to start or continue the journey of healthful living — while enjoying the recreational tranquility of nature and a deeper connection with God, your Maker.

Our Healing Framework

The NEWSTART Approach

Eight natural laws of health that form the foundation of every treatment, meal, and program at Heavenly Foretaste.

N
Nutrition

Whole plant-based foods that nourish and restore vitality from within.

E
Exercise

Invigorating movement to strengthen the body and elevate mood naturally.

W
Water

Hydrotherapy and pure water for cleansing and healing the body inside out.

S
Sunlight

The healing power of sunlight for vitamin D, energy, and mental clarity.

T
Temperance

Embracing the good and abstaining from all that harms body or mind.

A
Air

Fresh clean air — vital to oxygenate the blood and revitalize the mind.

R
Rest

Complete rest — physical, mental, and spiritual — to renew every aspect of life.

T
Trust in God

Faith and connection with your Maker as the ultimate source of all healing.

04

Lifestyle Disease Treatments & Consultations

⌄

This is the heartbeat of our ministry. We offer personalised consultations and natural, God-designed treatments for those living with non-communicable diseases — helping the body heal itself through NEWSTART principles rather than pharmaceutical dependency. We treat the root, not just the symptom.

  • Hydrotherapy (hot & cold water treatments)
  • Massage therapy for pain & circulation
  • Detox programs & cleansing protocols
  • Personalised nutrition & diet plans
  • Health screening & lifestyle assessments
  • Conditions treated: hypertension, diabetes, obesity, cancer, depression, hormonal imbalance, and more

Recreational Events

⌄

Recreation at Heavenly Foretaste is not idle leisure — it is intentional, restorative, and God-centred. Our recreational events are designed to reconnect guests with nature, with each other, and with their Maker — bringing joy, refreshment, and restoration to every part of the person.

  • Nature trail hikes through the Kentani hills
  • Team-building challenges & adventure activities
  • Reading retreats by water — books from spiritual library
  • Godly meditation & nature reflection walks
  • Group games, fellowship, and creative expression
  • Community picnics, events & outdoor gatherings

Industrial Skills for Youth

⌄

We believe that practical skills empower young people to be self-sustaining, dignified, and useful for God's kingdom. Our industrial skills training integrates hands-on learning into retreats and programs — giving youth something tangible to take home and build a livelihood from.

  • Sewing & crocheting (clothing & home goods)
  • Baking & plant-based food preparation
  • Floristry & flower arrangement
  • Cosmetic & natural product making
  • Carpentry & basic woodworking (males)
  • Plumbing, maintenance & electrical basics
  • Gardening, composting & food growing
Enquire About Skills Training
07

Elderly Health Relief Program

⌄

Our elderly program is offered with the utmost gentleness, dignity, and love. We recognise that many senior community members carry years of untreated health conditions and deserve personalised, compassionate care. This program is both in-retreat and mobile — we bring care directly to those who cannot travel.

  • Gentle massage therapy & pain relief treatments
  • Health screenings (blood pressure, glucose, weight)
  • Nutritional guidance for age-related conditions
  • Stress relief counselling & emotional support
  • Monthly home visits in the Kentani community
  • Gentle exercise programs for mobility & strength

The Annual Retreat Calendar

What a Retreat Year Looks Like

January – May

Planning, registration, and preparation for the mid-year Girls RESTART Retreat. Applications open, program developed, and team mobilised in prayer.

June / July

Girls RESTART Retreat — 5 to 7 days of mountain camping, faith, skills, and healing in Kentani.

August – October

Community outreach programs, health expos, and preparation for the end-of-year retreat. Registration opens for Healing in the Hills.

November / December

Healing in the Hills Retreat — restorative end-of-year experience for adults, couples, and families.
